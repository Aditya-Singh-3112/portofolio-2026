About

I work at the intersection of deep learning and systems engineering — the layer where a model that performs well on paper has to actually fit on the hardware in front of it. That's meant building memory orchestration frameworks that let large architectures train on consumer GPUs, and compression pipelines that shrink vision transformers for edge deployment without giving up the accuracy that made them worth building in the first place.

Most recently I was a Software Development Engineer Intern at Amazon, building AgentCore-based infrastructure used by thousands of internal users. Before that, my Master's research at IIIT Allahabad produced a patented offloading architecture (CAAO) and a thesis on pipelining it for high-throughput, VRAM-efficient training. I'm currently applying that same edge-constrained mindset to Applied Scientist work in on-device AI.
